# Photo credits – MUJ demo listings

The four demo parking spots around Manipal University Jaipur use real photos from **Wikimedia Commons**.
They are free to reuse under the Creative Commons licences below, with credit to the photographer.
The photos were cropped to 16:10 and resized to 900 px wide (allowed by the licences).
They show the Jaipur–Ajmer Road and typical Indian parking areas – they are **illustrations for demo listings**,
not photos of the exact parking spots.
